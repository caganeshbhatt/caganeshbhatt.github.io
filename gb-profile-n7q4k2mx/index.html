03 Case studies

How the outcomes were created.

Selected examples are anonymised to protect commercially sensitive information.

01

Turning close into a management tool

25 → 5 days
Situation
Monthly performance reporting reached leadership 25 days after month-end, limiting its value for active decision-making.
Action
Rebuilt the close cadence around clear ownership, standardised schedules and earlier resolution of material exceptions.
Outcome
Reduced the monthly close to five days—an 80% improvement—and moved management conversations closer to the operating reality.
02

Making the forecast dependable

85% → 97%
Situation
Forecasts were not consistently reflecting the latest business drivers, weakening confidence in forward-looking decisions.
Action
Introduced driver-based forecasting, clearer input accountability and a recurring actual-versus-forecast learning loop.
Outcome
Improved forecast accuracy by 12 percentage points, from 85% to 97%.
03

Resetting the operating cost base

₹2 Cr / month
Situation
The recurring cost base required a structural review—not another round of temporary, across-the-board cuts.
Action
Created decision-grade spend visibility, challenged run-rate assumptions with business owners and prioritised reductions by strategic value.
Outcome
Removed or reduced ₹2 crore of recurring monthly cost while protecting business-critical capability.
04

Converting insight into profitability

₹2.5 Cr / month
Situation
Headline growth did not provide enough clarity on which products, customers and operating choices were creating economic value.
Action
Connected P&L performance with unit economics and operating drivers, then translated the analysis into focused commercial actions.
Outcome
Improved monthly profitability by ₹2.5 crore.
